## 7 October 2026
{: #databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2-07oct2026}
{: release-note}

Enhanced Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) experience in the provisioning UI
 : The provisioning experience now includes an updated encryption configuration component for customer-managed encryption keys through {{site.data.keyword.keymanagementservicefull}}. This update provides a more consistent key management experience during deployment creation. Learn more about [{{site.data.keyword.keymanagementserviceshort}} integration](/docs/databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2?topic=databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2-key-protect&interface=ui) or provision a new [{{site.data.keyword.databases-for-elasticsearch}} Gen 2 deployment](https://cloud.ibm.com/databases/databases-for-elasticsearch/create) with customer-managed encryption enabled.

## 1 October 2026
{: #databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2-01oct2026}
{: release-note}

{{site.data.keyword.databases-for-elasticsearch}} Standard Plan is now available
:  The {{site.data.keyword.databases-for-elasticsearch}} Standard Plan offers the core functionalities of the Elasticsearch service available under an Elastic Basic license. For more information, see [Plan overview](/docs/databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2?topic=databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2-elastic-offerings&interface=ui).

## 30 Sep 2026
{: #databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2-30sep2026}
{: release-note}

{{site.data.keyword.databases-for-elasticsearch}} Gen 2 is now available in all VPC multizone regions
:  You can now deploy {{site.data.keyword.databases-for-elasticsearch}} Enterprise in all supported {{site.data.keyword.cloud}} VPC multizone regions (MZRs). This release adds support for Toronto (ca-tor), Tokyo (jp-tok), Osaka (jp-osa), and Sao Paulo (br-sao). For more information, see [Overview of Gen 1 and Gen 2](/docs/databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2?topic=databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2-overview-gen1-gen2#feature-differentiators).

## 17 September 2026
{: #databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2-17sep2026}
{: release-note}

{{site.data.keyword.databases-for-elasticsearch}} Gen 2 is now available in Dallas and London
:  The {{site.data.keyword.databases-for-elasticsearch}} Enterprise plan is now available in Dallas (us-south) and London (eu-gb). These regions are in addition to the existing regions Montreal (ca-mon), Chennai - Airtel (in-che), Mumbai (in-mum), Frankfurt (eu-de), Washington (us-east), Madrid (eu-es), and Sydney (au-syd).
:  For more information, see [Overview of Gen 1 and Gen 2](/docs/databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2?topic=databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2-overview-gen1-gen2#feature-differentiators).


## 10 September 2026
{: #databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2-10sep2026}
{: release-note}

{{site.data.keyword.databases-for-elasticsearch}} Gen 2 is now available in multiple regions
:  The {{site.data.keyword.databases-for-elasticsearch}} Enterprise plan is now available in Madrid (eu-es) and Sydney (au-syd), in addition to the existing regions Montreal (ca-mon), Chennai - Airtel (in-che), Mumbai (in-mum), Frankfurt (eu-de), and Washington (us-east). For more information, see [Overview of Gen 1 and Gen 2](/docs/databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2?topic=databases-for-elasticsearch-gen2-overview-gen1-gen2#feature-differentiators).
